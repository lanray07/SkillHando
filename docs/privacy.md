# SkillHando Privacy Policy

SkillHando is published by Lanray. For privacy questions or requests about your information, contact banksmi@mail.com.

## Your practice records

SkillHando does not require a SkillHando account. The app saves your practice notes, ratings, goals, preferences and optional photo and audio evidence in private storage on your device. The practice coach uses the bundled catalogue and your completed practice records on the device. The app has no advertising or developer analytics SDK, and Android app backup is disabled.

Open **More → Privacy settings** to delete your local practice records and attachments. You can also clear the app's storage using your device settings. If you choose to export or share a report, you control where it goes and how long the resulting copy is kept.

## Microphone and speech services

You can use the app without microphone access. If you choose to record practice evidence, the app saves that recording on your device. If you start a voice command, the app sends microphone input to your device's speech recognition service. Depending on your device and settings, that service may process audio online under its own privacy terms. SkillHando does not save the voice command audio. If spoken feedback is enabled, SkillHando sends the text to your device's text-to-speech service. This can include your active goal labels if you ask the app to read your goals aloud. The service may process the text on your device or online, depending on your selected engine and voice. You can turn off spoken guidance and revoke microphone access in device settings.

## Amazon Premium

Amazon handles payment and manages your purchase. SkillHando does not receive your payment card information. To activate or restore Premium, the app sends your Amazon Appstore user identifier and receipt identifier over HTTPS to SkillHando's verifier on Cloudflare Workers. The verifier sends those identifiers to Amazon to confirm the product and check whether the purchase was cancelled. Cloudflare processes the connecting IP address for rate limiting. The verifier has no receipt database and application request logging is disabled. Amazon and Cloudflare each handle their service records under their own privacy policies.

The app stores an Amazon account identifier and the expiry of its cached entitlement locally to support access between verification checks. Clearing app storage removes this local cache. Amazon retains its own purchase records.

## Support and requests

If you contact support, Lanray receives your email address and the information you include and uses them to respond and handle your request. We keep support messages as needed to respond and keep a record of the request. Please share only what is needed. Contact banksmi@mail.com to ask about your data or request access, correction or deletion where applicable. For information held by Amazon, use the privacy controls on your Amazon account.
