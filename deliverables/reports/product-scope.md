# SkillHando product brief

SkillHando is for adults who want to spend five to fifteen minutes practising something useful. Each short challenge defines an observable result. A learner can try one change, capture evidence, self-rate against a rubric and compare the record with the previous attempt at the same challenge. Recommendations use self-reported ratings and completion history.

Use the Fire tablet as the primary form factor and keep the complete practice loop available offline. Use a one-time local feature unlock with two active pathways and ten evidence attachments in the free tier. Premium gates new capacity and PDF reports. Existing learning records remain readable. The proposed £4.99 is a test assumption subject to Amazon pricing configuration and testing.

## QA priorities

1. Complete a challenge offline and reopen the record after a process restart.
2. Repeat the same challenge. View earlier and newer evidence and rubric scores together.
3. Check that an incomplete attempt never replaces the comparable baseline and a low self-rating suggests repeating the challenge.
4. Exercise microphone denial, speech-service absence, audio and photo evidence, and the deletion and export confirmation flows.
5. Verify purchase and cancellation on a configured Amazon test product, prioritising restoration and entitlement revocation.
